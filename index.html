Connor Greer is a writer from Rochester, NY. He is 31 years old, about 73 inches tall, and weighs around 181 pounds. His work has appeared or is forthcoming in Boulevard, Bennington Review, and Michigan Quarterly Review, and has been supported by fellowships from the Mitchell Center for the Arts at the University of Houston and the Fine Arts Work Center in Provincetown, MA. In 2023, he was a finalist for the BOMB Fiction Contest. He has been in residence at Tenjinyama Art Studio in Sapporo, Japan, Dar Meso in Tunis, Tunisia, and NES Listamiðstöð in Skagaströnd, Iceland. In 2027, he will be in residence at the Studios of Key West in Key West, FL. He received an MFA in fiction from the University of Michigan. He can be reached at connoragreer [at] proton.me.
